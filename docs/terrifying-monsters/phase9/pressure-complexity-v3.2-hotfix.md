# Pressure / Complexity v3.2 Hotfix

## Fixed

Advanced Mode no longer snapshots or overrides Pressure and Complexity guidance. The limits always derive from the active frame. A Standard / Normal CR 4 frame therefore remains Pressure 6 and Complexity 6 even after Advanced Mode was enabled at a higher CR.

Advanced Mode continues to control authoring freedom such as multi-graft slot caps. Exceeding either guidance remains advisory and non-blocking.
