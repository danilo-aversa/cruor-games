# Terrifying Monsters — Horror Graft Editorial Contract

## Purpose

A Horror Graft is the encounter's memorable sensory reveal. It changes how the scene feels and creates a short, readable state change, but it is not a second Attack Pattern and it cannot rely on arbitrary permanent punishment.

## Family requirement

The published catalog must provide at least one source-specific Horror Graft for each Monster Source Anchor:

- Decomposition;
- Jikininki;
- Wolf Spiders;
- Wax Death Masks.

A source cannot be considered editorially complete when its monsters can select the Horror slot only through a feature authored for another source.

## Publication rules

A publishable Horror Graft must:

1. produce an immediately recognizable sensory event;
2. specify when the reveal happens and whether it can repeat;
3. expose a visible or audible telegraph before the decisive effect;
4. provide a positioning answer;
5. provide a break condition or immunity window;
6. provide at least one non-damage response;
7. use bounded combat consequences;
8. compile cleanly at CR 1, 5, 10, and 15;
9. preserve verified structured-rule and renderer parity;
10. retain canonical Bestiary terminology when the underlying feature remains recognizably canonical.

## Slot boundaries

### Horror versus Body

Body defines what the creature physically is and what its anatomy can sustain. Horror defines what witnesses experience when that body is revealed.

Example:

- `Egg Carrier` creates physical eggs and brood resources;
- `Crawling Dread` is the once-per-encounter sight of the brood moving beneath the body.

### Horror versus Mind

Mind defines why the monster chooses targets and how it interprets the encounter. Horror affects witnesses; it does not define the monster's beliefs or priorities.

### Horror versus Attack Pattern

An Attack Pattern controls the offensive turn. A Horror Graft can deal modest damage or control space, but it cannot become the monster's primary DPR routine.

### Horror versus Twist

A Twist changes the rules or phase of the encounter. Horror is a sensory reveal with a bounded consequence. If the effect creates a new persistent combat state, it belongs in Twist.

## Prohibited designs

Do not publish a Horror Graft that is only:

- a generic Frightened rider added to an attack;
- permanent aging without a recovery subsystem;
- unavoidable campaign damage;
- a visual description with no table procedure;
- an aura with no readable boundary;
- a once-per-turn control loop with no immunity or escape;
- a duplicate of an existing Body, Mind, Attack, or Twist mechanic.
