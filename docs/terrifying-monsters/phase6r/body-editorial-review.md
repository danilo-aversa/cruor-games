# Phase 6R — Body Graft Editorial Review

## Outcome

All twelve existing Body Graft IDs are retained for preset and save compatibility. None was acceptable unchanged as a final authored product: each entry was rewritten, expanded, renamed, or given explicit CR progression.

The review produces fourteen authored abilities across twelve Grafts:

- ten single-ability chassis;
- two multi-ability bundles;
- three CR-scaled chassis.

No Body Graft was removed or merged in this pass because each source concept could be given a distinct physical role without duplicating another entry.

## Why all twelve remain

### Decomposition bodies

The four decomposition chassis now occupy different physical roles:

- **Swollen Corpse:** bloodied proximity hazard;
- **Bloat Hide:** defense-to-speed phase transition;
- **Corpse Mass:** immobile reach controller;
- **Shedding Skin:** restraint-resistant repositioner.

Their outputs no longer collapse into variations of “bloated corpse with a passive bonus.”

### Spirit body

**Anchored Spirit** is intentionally the only Body bundle that relies on a corpse object. It turns environmental remains into a defensive posture that players can destroy, burn, or move. The old Ethereal Sight remains as Grave Sight inside the bundle rather than occupying the entire Body slot by itself.

### Spider bodies

The five spider chassis now cover separate encounter functions:

- **Egg Carrier:** destructible summon resource;
- **Clinging Body:** wall and ceiling posture;
- **Web Sense:** connected-web awareness;
- **Barbed Chitin:** sustained-contact punishment;
- **Broodling:** expendable minion resource.

### Wax body

**Waxen Flesh** defines material preservation and heat suppression. The separate Weakness Graft remains responsible for the stronger dedicated fire exploit.

## Compatibility policy

Legacy IDs are unchanged. Existing presets, saves, registry references, and source anchors therefore continue to resolve. Published titles and mechanics are allowed to change because identity is carried by the stable Graft ID.

The review also updates:

- Ethereal Sight compatibility grants to expose corpse anchoring;
- Ethereal Sight anatomy grants to expose a spectral body;
- Egg Carrier mechanic metadata to remove the obsolete random-table model.

## Deferred work

This document closes only the Body family. It does not certify Mind, Movement, Horror, Twist, Weakness, Death, or Lair as editorially publishable. Those slots require the same decision register and publication gate before the final Public Hardening rerun.
