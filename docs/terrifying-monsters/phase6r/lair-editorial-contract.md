# Terrifying Monsters — Lair Effect Editorial Contract

## Purpose

A Lair Effect is not a global penalty pasted onto initiative count 20. It makes the environment participate in the encounter through one visible anchor, bounded area, route change, object, or terrain procedure that players can read and answer.

## Required structure

Every published Lair Effect must provide:

1. a stable Graft ID and source anchor;
2. explicit initiative count 20 timing;
3. one complete authored Lair Action;
4. a measurable area and range;
5. a visible environmental telegraph;
6. a positioning answer;
7. a finite duration, cap, or cleanup condition;
8. a non-damage interaction with the environment;
9. verified structured-rule and renderer parity.

## Family roles

Lair Effects may occupy one of these roles:

- **Temporary hazard:** a bounded area that lasts until the next initiative count 20.
- **Triggered terrain:** one or more visible one-use traps with finite targets.
- **Maintained prison:** a strong zone that prevents the monster from using another Lair Effect while maintained.
- **Persistent infrastructure:** capped terrain that remains until individual squares are cleared.
- **Ritual anchor:** an object or corpse whose fictional handling ends the effect.

## Scaling policy

CR scaling may alter radius, Cube size, or the number of finite anchors selected. It must not remove the safe route, erase the cleanup procedure, or create uncapped encounter-wide terrain.

Scaled IDs:

- `choking-air`;
- `corpse-pressure-room`;
- `funeral-silence-lair`;
- `broodmother-web-lair`.

## Wolf Spider separation policy

The three Wolf Spider Lair Effects must never collapse into interchangeable web penalties:

- `sticky-surroundings` creates temporary one-use restraint traps;
- `broodmother-web-lair` creates one maintained restraint prison;
- `dense-web-region` creates capped persistent terrain and vibration detection.

## Prohibited legacy patterns

- blanket disadvantage applied throughout the entire lair;
- noncanonical spellcasting ability checks for verbal components;
- 24-hour immunity to an ordinary Lair Action;
- persistent terrain with no cap or square-clearing procedure;
- undefined “small burst” damage;
- a selected object with no movement, destruction, ritual, or cleanup answer;
- surprised-target riders unrelated to the Lair Effect's primary identity.
