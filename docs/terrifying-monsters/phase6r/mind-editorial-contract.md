# Terrifying Monsters — Mind Graft Editorial Contract

## Purpose

A Mind Graft is not a generic intelligence bonus, skill proficiency, or isolated control rider. It defines **how the monster interprets the encounter**: what it notices, whom it prioritizes, what it believes, what it refuses to abandon, and which player actions can redirect that behavior.

The Attack Pattern answers **what the monster does offensively**. The Mind Graft answers **why it chooses that target, route, posture, or objective**.

## Publication requirements

Every publishable Mind Graft must provide:

1. **Behavioral fantasy** — a distinct obsession, cognitive structure, stolen identity, hunting logic, or perceptual model.
2. **Concrete routine** — a default plan and target-selection rule the GM can apply without inventing the behavior at the table.
3. **Observable telegraph** — players can infer the mental rule before or while it matters.
4. **Positioning answer** — movement, formation, light, cover, or route choice can alter the behavior.
5. **Break condition** — a defined event suppresses, transfers, exposes, or invalidates the rule.
6. **Non-damage answer** — deception, evidence, ritual action, object interaction, sensory obstruction, or social play can affect it.
7. **Bounded complexity** — the Mind Graft guides decisions without replacing the Attack Pattern or adding an entire second turn engine.
8. **Structured parity** — every authored ability must compile and render from structured rules at the tested CR checkpoints.

## Accepted forms

### Behavioral trait

One rule with a strong target or objective priority. Appropriate when the behavior is fully legible in one procedure.

### Behavioral bundle

Two or more independently readable procedures that form one mental system, such as:

- quarry selection + stalking action;
- brood priority + protective reaction;
- compulsion + feeding posture;
- perception model + focused observation.

### Pattern bundle

A behavioral bundle with explicit sequencing, retargeting, and termination conditions. It can guide the turn, but it must not duplicate the offensive sequence owned by the Attack Pattern.

## Prohibited shortcuts

A Mind Graft is not publishable when it is only:

- advantage on a skill;
- a flat passive-Perception bonus;
- a vague instruction to roleplay the monster as hungry or angry;
- an unconditional target priority with no player-facing way to manipulate it;
- a social disguise without a table procedure for investigation and exposure;
- a control effect detached from a visible belief, object, witness, or trigger;
- a summon, body mutation, or movement feature misplaced in Mind merely because instinct is mentioned.

## Scaling policy

CR scaling is appropriate only when the same mental concept must affect a larger battlefield or remain relevant against higher-tier parties. Valid scalar dimensions include:

- pursuit distance;
- bounded healing or sustain tied to the behavior;
- number of first-time witnesses or marked targets.

Target priority, identity, telegraph, and counterplay must remain stable across all bands.
