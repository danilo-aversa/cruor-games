# Terrifying Monsters — Lair Effect Editorial Review

## Executive decision

The seven migrated Lair Effects were technically compilable but not publishable as a coherent family. Several used encounter-wide penalties, one required a noncanonical spellcasting ability check, one referred to undefined burst damage, one granted 24-hour immunity to a maintained web, and the three Wolf Spider options overlapped without distinct tactical jobs.

The review retains every stable ID while rebuilding the family around bounded environmental procedures and explicit player interaction.

## Decisions

| Stable ID | Published title | Source | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `choking-air` | Choking Air | Decomposition | Rewrite and scale low-ground hazard |
| `corpse-pressure-room` | Pressure Corpses | Decomposition | Rewrite and scale corpse minefield |
| `funeral-silence-lair` | Funeral Silence | Jikininki | Rewrite and scale anchored silence |
| `graveyard-offerings-lair` | Graveyard Offerings | Jikininki | Rewrite as ritual lure |
| `sticky-surroundings` | Snapping Webs | Wolf Spiders | Rewrite as one-use web traps |
| `broodmother-web-lair` | Broodmother Web | Wolf Spiders | Rewrite and scale maintained prison |
| `dense-web-region` | Dense Webs | Wolf Spiders | Rewrite as persistent vibration terrain |

## Decomposition

### Choking Air

The effect is now visibly floor-bound and anchored to a corpse, drain, vent, or standing fluid. Breathing creatures risk reduced speed and reaction loss, but elevation, ventilation, leaving the zone, and not needing to breathe are complete answers. CR scaling changes only the radius.

### Pressure Corpses

The former undefined “small toxic burst” becomes a finite minefield. One, two, or three visible corpses are armed according to CR. Each can trigger only once and can be vented with Medicine or destroyed harmlessly with Fire or Radiant damage.

## Jikininki

### Funeral Silence

The noncanonical spellcasting ability check is removed. A visible funerary anchor creates a true bounded silence zone. Moving, covering, or consecrating the anchor ends it, and CR scaling changes only the radius.

### Graveyard Offerings

The lure now creates explicit forced movement around a selected corpse or grave good. Returning the anchor, moving it, or placing a culturally appropriate respectful offering ends the effect immediately.

## Wolf Spiders

### Snapping Webs

The encounter-wide Dexterity disadvantage is removed. Two visible web squares become one-use traps. Each can restrain only its first victim and can be cut or burned before it triggers.

### Broodmother Web

The maintained prison is now self-contained rather than referencing the Web spell. Escape checks, web-section statistics, Fire vulnerability, and the one-prison commitment are explicit. The monster cannot use another Lair Effect while the prison remains. CR scaling changes only Cube size.

### Dense Webs

The persistent terrain now has a cap of two active regions. It creates Difficult Terrain and communicates a creature's location to Web Walker creatures, but individual five-foot squares can be cleared with a blade or Fire damage.
