# Terrifying Monsters — Horror Graft Editorial Review

## Executive decision

The previous Horror catalog was not publishable as a family. It contained only `horrific-apparition`, so every source except Jikininki lacked a source-native Horror option.

The review expands the family from one to four Grafts while retaining the existing Jikininki ID. Each source now contributes a distinct sensory channel and player answer.

## Decisions

| Stable ID | Published title | Source | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stench` | Stench | Decomposition | Add canonical source Horror |
| `horrific-apparition` | Horrific Apparition | Jikininki | Rewrite and scale |
| `crawling-dread` | Crawling Dread | Wolf Spiders | Add source Horror |
| `wail` | Wail | Wax Death Masks | Add canonical source Horror |

## Stench

The feature uses a familiar D&D name and outcome: breathing creatures close to the monster risk the Poisoned condition. The revision makes the aura visible, keeps a 24-hour immunity after success, and formalizes wind, sealed breathing, distance, and non-breathing creatures as answers.

CR scaling changes only the aura radius:

- CR 0–4: 5 feet;
- CR 5–12: 10 feet;
- CR 13+: 15 feet.

## Horrific Apparition

The previous feature combined an at-will fear cone with vague supernatural aging that could require undefined campaign-level restoration. The revised version:

- uses Recharge 5–6;
- preserves the visual corpse-eater reveal;
- grants 24-hour immunity after a successful save;
- removes permanent aging and wasting;
- scales cone size and damage intensity only.

## Crawling Dread

Wolf Spiders already had eggs, brood behavior, brood retaliation, and brood death effects, but no Horror Graft. Crawling Dread does not create more creatures. It represents the moment witnesses see the brood moving beneath the opened body.

A witness can spend its reaction to avert its eyes and automatically succeed, but it loses sight of the monster until the start of its next turn. The player therefore chooses between exposure to the horror and temporary loss of visual information.

## Wail

Wax Death Masks had disguise, stolen identity, mask weakness, and death effects, but no dedicated sensory Horror Graft. Wail uses the canonical D&D title without copying the lethal banshee implementation.

The feature deals bounded psychic damage and brief fear. Creatures that cannot hear automatically succeed, while silence, deafness, sealed cover, and distance provide explicit answers.

## Catalog impact

- production Grafts: 90 → 93;
- support Grafts: 75 → 78;
- Horror Grafts: 1 → 4;
- authored ability definitions: 121 → 124;
- compiled Ability Model entries, including synthetic Multiattack: 136 → 139;
- source-native Horror coverage: 1/4 → 4/4.

No existing ID was removed or changed. Presets for the Decomposition setpiece, Broodmother, and Wax-Faced Stalker now select their source-native Horror Graft.
