# Terrifying Monsters — Death Effect Editorial Review

## Executive decision

The eight migrated Death Effects were technically valid but not publishable as a coherent family. Several were only post-combat clue prompts, one used a random roll against every remaining egg, terrain and condition durations were conflated, and the strongest burst lacked a reliable player-facing telegraph.

The review retains every stable ID while rebuilding the family around explicit aftermath procedures.

## Decisions

## Decomposition

### Corpse Bloom

The corpse now becomes a destroyable terrain anchor. Its bloom creates Difficult Terrain and suppresses healing for creatures that start their turns inside it. Fire or Radiant damage destroys the entire bloom, while actions can clear individual squares.

### Toxic Detonation

The canonical death burst remains, but it now uses an explicit death-burst damage role, a visible pressure telegraph, canonical failed/successful save outcomes, and radius-only CR scaling.

### Purge Flood

The previous rules treated Prone as though it lasted until the fluid was cleaned. The rewrite separates an instantaneous Prone result from a persistent slick. Players can clear safe squares with explicit materials and actions.

## Jikininki

### Spectral Dust

The former clue-only effect becomes the first Death bundle. Revealing Dust creates an immediate one-round anti-Invisibility and anti-Hide zone. Grave Trace then lets a creature study the settled dust to learn either the direction of the last feeding place or the identity of the last victim.

### Last Meal Memory

The selected witness receives a clear choice: spend a Reaction to reject both fear and information, or accept the memory, make the save, and receive one precise image regardless of success. Target selection and hearing requirements are explicit.

## Wolf Spiders

### Brood Burst

The random d20 roll against every egg is removed. A CR-scaled maximum of one, two, or three intact eggs hatches. Destroyed eggs and eggs exposed to Fire damage during the defined window cannot hatch.

### Death Cocoon

The former clue-only remains become the second bundle. Death Snare restrains the nearest creature with escape rules and finite silk statistics. Cocooned Remains creates a separate evidence object that can be cut open without destroying its contents.

## Wax Death Masks

### Last Face Curse

The mask now visibly selects witnesses. A target may spend its Reaction to avert its gaze or risk brief Frightened and reaction loss. Destroying or covering the mask remains a complete answer. CR scaling changes only the number of targets.

## Catalog impact

- stable Death Graft IDs: 8 retained;
- authored Death abilities: 8 → 10;
- multi-ability Death bundles: 0 → 2;
- CR-scaled Death Effects: 3 → 4;
- random mass-hatch procedures: 1 → 0;
- vague clue-only effects: 3 → 0.
