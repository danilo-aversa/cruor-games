# Body Graft Editorial Contract

## Purpose

A Body Graft changes the monster's **physical chassis**: what its body can endure, how it occupies space, which physical state it enters during the encounter, or which material feature players can directly exploit.

A publishable Body Graft is not merely a standard creature trait placed in the Body slot. It must materially change at least one of these encounter dimensions:

1. **Durability state** — armor, resistances, thresholds, healing, or destructible body resources.
2. **Spatial body** — reach, attachment, immobility, bodily contact, or interaction with terrain.
3. **Phase behavior** — a visible bodily transition tied to damage or another state change.
4. **Physical resource** — eggs, corpses, removable tissue, or another targetable part of the body.
5. **Embodied counterplay** — a physical tell and a concrete way to suppress, relocate, break, or exploit the body.

## Boundary with other slots

- **Attack** defines the monster's normal offensive routine.
- **Movement** defines voluntary traversal and repositioning patterns.
- **Body** may alter where the creature can stand or how it reacts to displacement, but it should do so because of anatomy or material state.
- **Weakness** defines a dedicated vulnerability or disabling answer. A Body Graft may contain a suppression window, but it must not replace the Weakness slot's primary purpose.
- **Twist** defines an encounter revelation or rule inversion. A Body phase is acceptable only when it is a predictable consequence of the physical chassis rather than a hidden narrative reversal.

## Required authoring fields

Every Body Graft must define:

- Type: `traitBundle`;
- fantasy;
- tactical role;
- signature behavior;
- at least four recognition tags;
- one or more authored abilities;
- explicit Monster Frame fit;
- balance, complexity, counterplay, and spike-risk profiles;
- all four counterplay channels:
  - telegraph;
  - positioning answer;
  - break condition;
  - non-damage answer;
- an editorial decision and rationale.

## Bundle rule

A Body Graft becomes a multi-ability bundle only when the body contains multiple independently readable game objects or procedures. A second ability must not exist merely to split one paragraph into two controls.

Approved Body bundles in this review:

- **Anchored Spirit** — perception plus corpse inhabitation;
- **Egg Carrier** — targetable clutch plus hatching cycle.

## CR scaling rule

CR progression is authored only when increasing CR should change the actual body behavior, not merely its numerical budget.

Approved CR-scaled Body Grafts:

- **Swollen Corpse** — expanding rupture radius and high-tier reaction denial;
- **Egg Carrier** — clutch size and hatch cadence;
- **Shadow Skin** — stealth, conditional invisibility, then sustained darkness invisibility.

All other Body Grafts remain static because their chassis rule stays readable and relevant across CRs without adding additional tracking.

## Publication gate

The Body family is publishable only if all entries:

- have an independent reason to occupy the Body slot;
- are not duplicated by another Body Graft;
- render every authored mechanic in the stat block;
- compile at CR 1, 5, 10, and 15;
- pass schema and rule-parity validation without warning;
- expose meaningful player-facing counterplay;
- preserve preset and legacy ID compatibility.
