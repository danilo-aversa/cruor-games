# Terrifying Monsters — Graft Naming Review

## Final editorial decision

Attack Patterns use evocative parent names. Their individual actions keep their canonical or descriptive names in the stat block. Body, Mind, and Movement retain the names approved in the canonical comparison pass. Horror preserves canonical names for Stench and Wail while using direct source-specific names for Horrific Apparition and Crawling Dread.
