# Terrifying Monsters — Movement Graft Editorial Contract

A publishable Movement Graft defines a **repeatable route, approach, escape, or repositioning pattern**. It is not publishable when it merely grants a standard speed, repeats a class feature, or provides teleportation without a visible spatial answer.

## Required qualities

Every Movement Graft must:

- explain when the GM should use the movement option;
- identify a route, destination, landing space, surface, line, shadow, or sight condition;
- expose its intended route before or during resolution;
- provide telegraph, positioning, break-condition, and non-damage counterplay;
- remain distinct from Body posture, Mind target priority, and Attack Pattern damage routines;
- compile at CR 1, 5, 10, and 15;
- scale only dimensions that materially need CR growth;
- use a one- or two-word published name with no dash punctuation.

## Slot boundary

- **Body** defines what surfaces or postures the anatomy can physically sustain.
- **Mind** defines why the creature chooses a target or objective.
- **Movement** defines the route and procedure used to change position.
- **Attack Pattern** defines the offensive sequence after the creature arrives.

A Movement Graft may support an attack, but it must remain useful and readable as a positioning procedure rather than becoming a second Attack Pattern.
