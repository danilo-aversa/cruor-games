# Terrifying Monsters — Phase 6R Mind Editorial Review

## Summary

The eleven Mind Grafts were technically valid after Phase 6, but most still reflected the legacy model: a single paragraph, generic passive bonus, or narrative prompt without a complete table procedure. This review keeps every stable ID while rebuilding the family around explicit behavioral logic.

## Family-level changes

- All eleven Grafts now carry a concrete `procedure` routine with a default plan and target-selection rule.
- Six Grafts are multi-ability bundles rather than overloaded single paragraphs.
- Three Grafts use authored CR progression: `pressure-agony`, `shame-hunger`, and `borrowed-face`.
- Every Graft exposes telegraph, positioning, break-condition, and non-damage counterplay channels.
- Generic bonuses were replaced with decisions: `Hunter` is no longer only Stealth advantage, and `Hundred-Eyed` is no longer only a passive-Perception modifier.
- Misplaced mechanics were removed: `Maternal Swarm Instinct` no longer spawns a brood or grants flat damage, because those belong to Body, summon, or Attack systems.
- Social features now include executable procedures: both disguise exposure and stolen-life collapse specify evidence, witnesses, and encounter consequences.

## Distinctions preserved

### Dead Impostor vs. Stolen Life

The Masquerade is an **infiltration capability**: what the impostor can mimic and how investigators expose the disguise. The Delusion is an **obsession and vulnerability**: what identity the monster believes, which anchor it protects, and what public contradiction does to its behavior.

### Night Hunter vs. Shadow Skin

Night Hunter changes **decision logic and route preference** around light. Shadow Skin changes **physical visibility and defense**. They can coexist without duplicating one another.

### Brood Guard vs. Egg Carrier

The Body Graft creates and exposes a physical clutch. The Mind Graft can protect any designated brood anchor and defines the guardian's target priority and reaction movement. It remains usable without requiring the Body Graft.

### Patient Hunter vs. Attack Pattern

Patient Hunter determines whom the predator stalks, when it hides, and when the hunt ends. The selected Attack Pattern still determines the attacks performed after the predator reaches its quarry.
