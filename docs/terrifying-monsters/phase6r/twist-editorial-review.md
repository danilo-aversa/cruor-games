# Terrifying Monsters — Twist Graft Editorial Review

## Summary

The legacy Twist family contained twelve stable IDs, but most entries were still passive modifiers, hidden random triggers, or unbounded combat bonuses. All twelve IDs are preserved for compatibility. Every entry has been rewritten or explicitly retained under the Twist editorial contract.

## Major corrections

### Random escalation removed

The old Decomposition Twists often required a hidden d6 after taking a particular damage type. These have been replaced by:

- visible Pressure markers;
- reaction economy;
- a public one-turn fuse;
- player-controlled disarm or redirection procedures.

### Permanent stacking removed

Flesh Harvest no longer grants cumulative attack, damage, and Armor Class bonuses until dawn. It now creates one finite Sated phase supported by temporary hit points. Removing those temporary hit points ends the phase.

### Ambush spike reduced

Horrific Assault no longer turns hits against surprised creatures into automatic critical hits. The false corpse can be identified through Medicine, Investigation, detection magic, or cautious interaction. A missed inspection grants repositioning and brief reaction denial, not automatic double damage.

### Player-triggered retaliation clarified

Enrage is deterministic and lasts through the end of the monster's next turn. The party decides who destroys the egg, from where, and after which control effects have been prepared.

### Terrain-changing Twists formalized

Siege Monster, Web Architect, and Corrosive Web now alter the battlefield through bounded, visible procedures:

- one major breach;
- destructible web anchors and walls;
- one acid release per round when a web is destroyed.

### Mask phase made readable

Changing Mask announces Mourner, Accuser, or Saint when the creature first becomes bloodied. Fire suppresses the benefit temporarily, while a grappled or restrained creature can have its mask physically removed.
