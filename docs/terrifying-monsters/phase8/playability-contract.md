# Phase 8 — Playability Contract

A publishable graft must be executable during play without reconstructing author intent.

The gate requires:

- successful compilation at CR 1, 5, 10, and 15;
- a visible telegraph;
- a positioning answer;
- a finite break condition;
- a non-damage answer;
- declared complexity no lower than authored decision, sequence, branch, or tracking load;
- an executable default procedure for behavior-facing slots;
- bounded objects, zones, counters, and summon counts;
- soft compatibility warnings for combinations that are legal but likely to overload tracking.

The review judges GM operation, player readability, and meaningful counterplay separately from mathematical balance.
